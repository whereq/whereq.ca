# WhereQ App Hub v1.9.6 — map-toolbar

> The map's tool bar does what its buttons say, and every one of them tells you what it will do.

## What's in this release

- **Every button in the map's tool bar now works, and says what it does.**
  The bar holds a hand, five shape tools, a ruler, group, ungroup, delete,
  save, capture, export and import. Several of them were placeholders that
  opened the wrong dialog — the delete button opened the "Save Overlays"
  form. Each one now does its own job.

- **Hovering a button tells you what it will do.** Every button carries a
  tooltip that appears above it, and the same words are its accessible
  name, so the bar is usable with a screen reader as well as a mouse.

- **Clicking a shape selects it; shift-click picks more than one.** A
  plain click selects that shape and lets go of the others; a shift-click
  adds a shape to the selection or takes it out again. That is what makes
  "group these two shapes" possible. The orange outline is painted from
  the selection itself, so a shape you delete can no longer keep a stale
  highlight.

- **Delete asks first, then removes what you picked.** A short warning
  dialog says how many shapes will be taken off the map and that the saved
  copies in "My Map" are kept — deleting a shape from the drawing is not
  the same as deleting a saved map.

- **Group and Ungroup work over the selection.** Grouping puts the shapes
  you picked under one name; the shapes stay on the map exactly as they
  were. Ungrouping takes them out again. A group is a container in the
  app's own model, so the GeoJSON export writes the shapes inside it — and
  nothing is lost by grouping.

- **Save asks for a name and nothing more.** The description is optional
  now, and "My Map" refreshes itself as soon as the drawing is saved.

- **Capture Map saves a picture of the map.** The camera button writes a
  PNG of the current map view, `whereq-map-YYYY-MM-DD.png`. It is taken
  from the map's own drawing, so it costs no extra API quota and needs no
  separate key.

- **The ruler measures.** Two clicks give the distance between the points,
  shown in a bubble on the map and in the notice under the tool bar —
  metres under a kilometre, kilometres above it.

- **The workspace's copy button copies.** A saved shape can be duplicated
  under its own name, and its delete button removes it from "My Map".

## Notes

- The release notes live in `docs/releases/v1.9.6-map-toolbar-2026-11-21.md`.
- The git tag for this release is `v1.9.6`.
- Status is `shipped`, set by `--status`.
- Picking a shape needs the hand tool. With a shape tool still active, a
  click on empty map starts a *new* shape — several shapes can be drawn in
  a row, which is deliberate.
- A group has no shape of its own on the map, so it cannot be clicked. The
  ungroup button therefore takes apart the selected group, or the most
  recent one.
