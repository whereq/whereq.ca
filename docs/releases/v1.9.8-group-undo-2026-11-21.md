# WhereQ App Hub v1.9.8 — group-undo

> Undo now takes back a grouping and an ungrouping as well as a deletion.

## What's in this release

- **Undo takes back the last change of any kind.** The Undo button and
  Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac) now undo a deletion, a grouping or an ungrouping
  — whichever you did last. The button's tooltip says "Undo the last
  change" now, and the notice tells you which change was taken back.

- **Undoing a grouping takes the group apart again.** The shapes go back to
  the exact places they held, so the drawing keeps its order and the
  exported GeoJSON file keeps the same feature order it had before you
  grouped them.

- **Undoing an ungroup puts the group back together.** The group is back
  in the drawing with its name and its description, and the "take a group
  apart" button is live again straight away.

- **The shapes come back where they belong.** Undoing a grouping leaves
  the shapes loose again, so you can pick them one at a time and delete
  them on their own — which you cannot do while they are inside a group.

- **The changes are taken back in the order you made them.** Group the
  shapes, take the group apart, undo twice: first the group comes back,
  then the shapes are loose again.

## Notes

- The release notes live in `docs/releases/v1.9.8-group-undo-2026-11-21.md`.
- The git tag for this release is `v1.9.8`.
- Status is `shipped`, set by `--status`.
- Grouping and ungrouping never take the shapes off the map — only the
  drawing's own list changes. Undoing them therefore leaves the map alone,
  exactly as it should.
- The app still remembers the last 20 changes. Loading a saved map, or
  leaving the map page, clears them.
- A shape that is inside a group cannot be picked on its own, so it cannot
  be deleted or highlighted on its own — take the group apart first.
